# AmbitionBox V3 copywriting

Write persuasive product copy from a true user tension and a provable AmbitionBox benefit. Treat clarity, specificity, and emotional truth as creative constraints.

## Read before writing

1. Read `context/COPY.md`, the relevant screen contract, and the active change brief.
2. Read `context/PRODUCT.md` when the task changes a promise or capability.
3. Read `context/FEEDBACK.md` for approved and rejected language patterns.
4. Read [references/DANDAD_COPY_PRINCIPLES.md](references/DANDAD_COPY_PRINCIPLES.md) when creating a new pitch, headline, or campaign-level direction.

## Build the copy

1. Write a one-sentence brief containing the audience, human tension, one proposition, desired action, and supporting evidence.
2. Generate three to five distinct creative territories. Change the underlying idea or tension, not just adjectives.
3. Select a territory by testing truth, clarity, benefit, distinctiveness, product fit, and stage fit.
4. Write in this order: headline, supporting explanation, proof labels, CTA, trust boundary.
5. Separate creating from editing. Cut repetition, abstract language, internal terminology, and cleverness that competes with the idea.
6. Read the result aloud. Prefer familiar words and natural spoken rhythm.

## Fit the interface

- Give each screen one dominant thought and each control one clear consequence.
- Keep a mobile headline to roughly two or three lines at 390px when possible.
- Keep supporting copy short enough to scan before the proof or action.
- Do not repeat the headline in the proof, CTA, or adjacent helper text.
- Name providers only where provider choice or source attribution matters.
- Put limitations and permission boundaries beside the claim or action they qualify.
- Use direct, verb-led CTAs. Provider icons may identify provider actions.
- Preserve the user's optionality without letting reassurance overwhelm the pitch.

## Protect AmbitionBox truth

- Keep onboarding focused on job search, not the user's entire career.
- Present intentional fit, company truth, and guided action together when relevant.
- Never imply that sign-in connects an inbox.
- Never imply that AmbitionBox sent, applied, or verified something it only drafted, inferred, or simulated.
- Avoid generic AI claims, unexplained intelligence, empty superlatives, and indiscriminate phrases such as “every opportunity.”
- Do not introduce “Career Assistant” or “Career Copilot” during onboarding.

## Final audit

Confirm that the copy earns attention without exaggeration, expresses one defensible opinion, makes the benefit concrete, sounds human, works with the visual, fits the UI, labels the action clearly, and preserves evidence, privacy, and simulation boundaries.

Record durable owner approvals and rejections in `context/FEEDBACK.md`. Nothing becomes approved or locked because it was implemented.
