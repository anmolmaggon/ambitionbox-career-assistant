✕
Never mass-apply. Bulk applying converts at 0.5% and fuels the doom loop.
✕
No public candidate score. Verdicts are private, explainable, advisory.
✕
No promises needing employer cooperation. We infer from signals employers already emit.
✕
Employer money never touches verdicts. The same wall reviews already keep.
✕
Never paywall the truth. Tracking, verdicts, and the digest are free, always.
✕
Consent is existential. Plain-language, revocable, DPDP-first from day zero.

Seven predecessors tried candidate advocacy with scores and feedback promises. Every one pivoted to employers, got absorbed, or died. The refusals are how we don't join them.
