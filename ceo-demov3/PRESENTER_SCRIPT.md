# CEO Demo V3 Presenter Script

## Full version — approximately 10 minutes

### 0:00–0:55 — Start with the moment every jobseeker wants

Open `/demo` on a desktop-width browser and click **Start golden path**.

“Let’s start at the moment every jobseeker is working toward.”

Tap the AmbitionBox notification. Let **This is the moment you’ve been working towards** breathe, then pause as **Congratulations, Arjun. You got the offer** and the ₹28L offer card arrive.

“AmbitionBox detects the offer, but the number is only the beginning. The real question is whether this is the right move for Arjun.”

Click **Understand my offer**.

“In one view, AmbitionBox surfaces the company reality, Arjun’s negotiation power, and what moving from Pune to Bengaluru changes.”

Use the presenter control outside the phone to click **Rewind to the beginning**.

### 0:55–2:35 — First open becomes a live career journey

“A few weeks earlier, this is Arjun’s first app open. AmbitionBox begins with a point of view: focus on what fits, know what is real, and get the guidance to give the right opportunity your best shot.”

The promise and setup now live on one screen. Point to the three concise benefits, then show that AmbitionBox has detected Arjun’s Naukri profile on the device. Choose **Continue with Naukri** to demonstrate profile, résumé, skills, and preference autofill without repeating an account-confirmation step.

“Naukri makes this one tap. Profile, résumé, and preferences arrive as a draft; Arjun reviews and edits everything before AmbitionBox uses it. Google and Apple remain full alternatives with Naukri, résumé, or a guided conversational setup.”

Review the imported profile and confirm preferences. Continue to the email screen.

“Only now—after the value is clear—do we ask for the email Arjun uses to apply. It is optional, read only, and limited to job-search messages.”

Choose the simulated Google account. Let the scan run briefly, then use **Skip scan**.

“AmbitionBox finds 15 applications: 3 need attention, 4 are waiting, and 8 are closed. But it does not land Arjun in a database. It carries the highest-value signal into Home.”

Click **Show my next move**.

### 2:35–3:15 — Home ranks the next useful action

“Home is a live command center. The PhonePe reply owns the hierarchy, the journey rail keeps Explore, Applications, Interviews, and Offer connected, and AmbitionBox can answer questions from the context it genuinely has.”

Click **Send reply**.

“Once that action is complete, Juspay becomes the strongest next opportunity. Every action changes what the assistant recommends.”

### 3:15–4:20 — Naukri adds transparent profile context

Open **Matches**, then **Connect Naukri**.

“The future connection is read-only. Arjun reviews his Pune location, Bengaluru preference, target pay, work mode, and the Jaipur home-city detail used only for move estimates.”

Confirm the profile and preferences.

“Juspay rises because role, pay, location, work mode, and evidence now work together.”

### 4:00–5:05 — One feed explains every opportunity

Click **See ranked matches**, then open Juspay.

“Juspay is an 89% Preference Match—the role fits what Arjun wants. Profile Readiness is 10/15—the résumé does not yet prove everything the role needs. Those are deliberately different measures.”

### 5:05–6:15 — Improve evidence without inventing it

Click **Improve my application**, use the demo evidence, and review it.

“AmbitionBox turns Arjun’s real example into structured evidence: his system, ownership, and a 31% reliability result. It never invents the metric.”

Tailor the résumé. Choose **Not quite** on the Java question.

“Readiness reaches 14/15, but the unconfirmed Java gap remains visible. Trust matters more than a perfect score.”

Return to the job and click **See my next move** to land back on Home.

### 6:15–7:45 — The invite says nothing. We decode it.

On Home, click **Jump ahead 3 days**. The interview card arrives in the priority slot.

Open it with **See what to expect**.

“This is the whole email. A role, a Tuesday, sixty minutes, a Meet link, and a first name. That is what everyone gets — and it is the actual problem. It does not say what the round is.”

Point at the inference block, then the confirmation choices.

“AmbitionBox worked out who Nikhil is, that this is round one of four, and that it is most likely a hiring-manager conversation — fifty-one of sixty-three reports. Notice it shows the confidence rather than asserting it, and then asks Arjun to confirm. We would rather ask than guess.”

Click **That matches**, then **Decode this interview**.

“Two blocks, deliberately separated. Everything a candidate could find themselves with about three hours of searching — the talk, the blog posts, the public headline. And everything only AmbitionBox has: sixty-three interview reports, eight hundred and forty-seven reviews filtered to this org, Naukri team data, and Arjun’s own confirmed evidence. We never touch anyone’s private profile or job-search activity, and the screen says so.”

Point at the inversion card.

“Most companies screen with coding first. Juspay opens with the manager. Fifty-one of sixty-three reports — and twelve say otherwise, which we also show. That single fact changes what Arjun does tonight.”

Tap **Incident ownership** to open the real excerpts, close the sheet, then click **Where my evidence stands**.

“Three of the five themes he can already prove, from evidence he confirmed himself two chapters ago. One is a framing problem, not a knowledge gap. And Java he cannot prove — so the system refuses to write him a Java story and coaches a truthful answer instead. His earlier ‘Not quite’ is still remembered here.”

Click **Practise the most likely question**, use the demo answer, and get coaching.

“The coaching is graded against what he actually typed — type nothing and it says so rather than praising him. The line it adds is attributed: seventeen of fifty-one reports say the candidates who advanced showed judgement under pressure.”

Complete practice. Note the derived metrics and the three questions to ask.

“He walks in as an evaluator, not an examinee — and those are the same three things he would have to verify before accepting an offer.”

Click **Jump to offer day**. The AmbitionBox notification returns; tap it before letting the celebratory ₹28L card land.

### 7:45–9:45 — Turn the offer into a life decision

Pause briefly on the full-screen offer, then click **Understand my offer**.

“Company reality comes first. Reviews support learning and culture, while work-life balance, team hybrid norms, workload, and variable payout still need verification.”

Click **Ask a Juspay employee anonymously**, review the question, and send the simulated request.

“AmbitionBox matches Arjun to a verified backend employee with similar relocation context. Neither identity nor Arjun’s résumé is shared. The reply is clearly employee experience—not official company policy.”

Use the response in the decision and return to the offer insights.

“Negotiation power is next. Juspay has chosen Arjun, the role range reaches ₹30L, his payments evidence is relevant, and the role requires relocation.”

Point out the move estimate: approximately ₹39k more monthly headroom, ₹13k higher living costs, and a ₹90k one-time relocation estimate. These assumptions are editable and explicitly approximate.

Click **Prepare my response**.

“The draft prioritises ₹30L or more fixed pay, then relocation support and team-specific hybrid clarification. Arjun can edit and copy it. Nothing is sent or accepted automatically.”

### 9:45–10:05 — Close

“AmbitionBox no longer stops at helping someone find a job. It helps them understand the company, evaluate the life change, and use their evidence to make a stronger decision.”

## Short version — approximately four and a half minutes

1. **0:00–0:35:** Notification, full-screen offer, and the three decision priorities.
2. **0:35–1:05:** Rewind; show Gmail trust and the 15-application result.
3. **1:05–1:35:** Show Home’s PhonePe action and Juspay reranking.
4. **1:35–2:10:** Show the multi-source feed and separate 89% Preference Match from 10/15 Profile Readiness.
5. **2:10–2:45:** Show confirmed evidence, résumé at 14/15, and the honest Java gap.
6. **2:45–3:20:** Open the vague invite, confirm the inferred round, and show the decoded loop, the interviewer, and the two source blocks.
7. **3:20–4:10:** Return to the offer; show company signals, negotiation power, anonymous employee perspective, and the reviewed draft.
8. **4:10–4:20:** “One connected journey—from scattered evidence to an informed career decision.”

## Recovery notes

- The presenter rewind appears outside the phone at desktop widths of 900px and above.
- Use `/demo` to reset or jump to any chapter.
- Every offer and prep state has a deterministic `stage` URL. Prep also takes `round=open|confirmed|corrected|unsure` and `java=open|yes|no`.
- If the round-confirmation choice is missed, **Decode this interview** stays disabled. That is deliberate — tap one of the three options.
- Gmail, Naukri, employee matching, employee replies, and the interviewer research are simulations; no real account or person is contacted.
- The interviewer is fictional. If asked what is real: interview reports and employee reviews are AmbitionBox first-party data, public footprint is what any candidate could search, and no private profile or job-search activity is ever used.
