# AmbitionBox Career Assistant — CEO Demo V2 Context

## Product thesis

Once AmbitionBox understands a candidate’s career context and inbox, the candidate should not have to manage the mechanics of a job search. AmbitionBox monitors, prepares, and chases; the candidate supplies judgment, approval, or facts the system cannot know.

## Canonical story

- Arjun Mehta, Senior Backend Engineer at Razorpay, 6 years, Bengaluru.
- Current compensation ₹15L; target ₹22L+.
- Juspay Senior Backend Engineer: hybrid, ₹24–30L, 89% Preference Match.
- Evidence improved from 10/15 to 14/15 before this story; 3+ years of Java ownership remains unconfirmed.
- Gmail has already organised 15 applications: 3 need Arjun, 4 are monitored, 8 are closed.
- The story follows Juspay from a five-day silence through follow-up, recruiter clarification, interview preparation, and a ₹28L offer.

## Trust contract

- Gmail is a deterministic simulation; no real account is accessed.
- Prepared and sent are distinct states.
- Every outbound message exposes the exact editable text and requires explicit approval.
- The first send explains a scoped permission that applies only to messages Arjun approves.
- The canonical Java branch is “Not quite”; confirmed payments and Kafka evidence are used without hiding the gap.

## Visual system

The application timeline and agent activity spine are the product’s signature. Space Grotesk carries decisive headlines, Figtree carries product copy, and monospace utility text attributes time and sources. The interface is a quiet career control room, not a chat surface or card dashboard.
