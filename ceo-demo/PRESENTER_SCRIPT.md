# CEO Demo Presenter Script

## Full version — approximately 8–10 minutes

### 0:00–0:40 — Frame the problem

Open `/demo`.

“A jobseeker’s journey is fragmented across inboxes, job platforms, company research, résumés, and interview notes. AmbitionBox already owns the company-intelligence moment. This prototype shows how we become the intelligence layer for the entire journey—understanding what is happening, evaluating what matters, and helping the candidate act.”

Click **Start golden path**.

### 0:40–1:50 — Gmail creates the journey automatically

On the trust sheet:

“We do not force an integration during onboarding. We ask only when the value is obvious. Arjun’s tracker is empty, so Gmail can turn existing job emails into a useful journey.”

Point out **Only job-search emails**, **Read only**, and **You stay in control**. Choose the simulated Google account. Let the scan run briefly, then use **Skip scan** if necessary.

“In seconds, AmbitionBox finds 15 applications: 3 need attention, 4 are waiting, and 8 are closed. This is not manual tracking—it is reconstructed from the evidence the candidate already has.”

Click **Open my tracker**, then open **PhonePe**.

### 1:50–2:40 — Home becomes an action hub

“The tracker identifies a recruiter reply. Home now ranks that action above passive browsing. The assistant drafts a response, but nothing sends until Arjun reviews it.”

Click **Send reply**.

“Once the action is complete, Home reranks immediately. Juspay becomes the best next opportunity. This cause-and-effect loop is the product: every action changes what AmbitionBox recommends next.”

### 2:40–3:55 — Naukri adds profile context

Open **Matches**, then **Connect Naukri**.

“AmbitionBox can already suggest roles, but Naukri adds the latest skill and preference context. This future one-tap connection is transparent: profile data is read-only, and Arjun confirms everything before ranking changes.”

Click **Connect in one tap**, review Arjun’s profile, then **Review and edit preferences**. Point out target salary and selectable work modes. Confirm.

“Juspay rises three places because the ranking now understands role, pay, location, work mode, and current skills.”

### 3:55–5:05 — One feed explains every opportunity

Click **See ranked matches**.

“This is not another undifferentiated job list. It combines Naukri, AmbitionBox, recruiter email, and company-career sources. Every card explains where it came from, Preference Match, Profile Readiness, pay, and company signals.”

Open the first **Senior Backend Engineer** role.

“Juspay is an 89% Preference Match: the job fits what Arjun wants. But Profile Readiness is only 10/15: his current evidence does not yet prove everything the role needs. These are intentionally different measures.”

### 5:05–6:20 — The assistant improves evidence without inventing it

Click **Improve my application**, then **Use demo evidence** and **Review my evidence**.

“The assistant asks one focused question and converts Arjun’s real answer into structured evidence: the system, his ownership, and a 31% reliability result. It never invents a metric.”

Click **Tailor my résumé for Juspay**.

“Readiness moves from 10/15 to 14/15. The strongest evidence is now where a recruiter will look.”

Choose **Not quite** on the Java question.

“This branch is important: if Arjun cannot honestly confirm three years of Java ownership, the product leaves the gap visible. Trust is more valuable than a perfect score.”

Click **Return to job**, then **Prepare for interview**.

### 6:20–7:35 — Interview preparation uses the whole context

Click **Jump ahead 3 days**.

“Gmail detects Juspay’s interview invitation. Tracker and Home update automatically.”

Click **Build my prep plan**.

“The plan combines the specific role, AmbitionBox interview reports, themes from 847 employee reviews, and Arjun’s confirmed résumé evidence.”

Start the practice, use the demo answer, and click **Get coaching**.

“The coaching is not generic. It recognizes what Arjun covered and gives one precise improvement: explain why state must be persisted before an external side effect.”

Complete the practice and click **Jump to offer day**.

### 7:35–8:50 — The offer becomes an informed decision

Pause on “You got the offer, Arjun.”

“This is the emotional payoff: a ₹28L offer from Juspay.”

Click **Understand my offer**.

“AmbitionBox turns the document into a decision. ₹28L is 87% above current pay, 27% above target, and within the ₹24–30L market range. We separate fixed, variable, and the one-time joining bonus, then identify a credible negotiation opportunity.”

Scroll to the draft.

“Arjun can edit and copy the message. Nothing is sent without review.”

### 8:50–9:20 — Close

“The through-line is simple: AmbitionBox understands the journey, evaluates each opportunity with trusted intelligence, and helps the candidate take the next action. The result is not a collection of tools. It is one career copilot with memory, context, and trust.”

## Four-minute version

Keep `/demo` available and use browser Back after each chapter jump.

3. **1:05–1:35 — Chapter 2:** Show PhonePe as the highest-value action and describe the Juspay rerank after review.
4. **1:35–2:10 — Chapter 4:** Show the four-source feed and distinguish 89% Preference Match from 10/15 Profile Readiness.
5. **2:10–2:50 — Chapter 5:** Show evidence extraction and the tailored résumé at 14/15. Emphasize the honest Java correction.
6. **2:50–3:20 — Chapter 6:** Show the Juspay-specific system-design coaching.
7. **3:20–3:50 — Chapter 7:** Reveal ₹28L, market comparison, and the editable unsent negotiation draft.
8. **3:50–4:00 — Close:** “One connected journey, powered by AmbitionBox intelligence and user-controlled evidence.”

## Recovery notes

- If a live interaction is interrupted, use browser Back to return to `/demo`, then open the relevant chapter.
- Use **Reset all demo data** before a fresh full run.
- The scan can be skipped without changing the result.
- Every chapter preset is deterministic; no network account is required.
