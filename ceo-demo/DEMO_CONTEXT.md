# AmbitionBox Jobseeker Assistant — CEO Demo Context

## Product thesis

AmbitionBox becomes the intelligence layer across a candidate's job search: it understands the journey, evaluates opportunities with credible company context, and helps the candidate take the next best action.

## Canonical candidate

- Arjun Mehta, Senior Backend Engineer at Razorpay
- 6 years of experience, Bengaluru
- Current compensation: ₹15L; target: ₹22L+
- Prefers senior backend roles and remote or light-hybrid work

## Canonical opportunity

- Senior Backend Engineer at Juspay
- Found through Naukri; Bengaluru; hybrid; full time; 6–9 years
- Salary range ₹24–30L; AmbitionBox rating 4.0 from 847 reviews
- Preference Match: 89%
- Initial Profile Readiness: 10/15, improving to 14/15 after résumé tailoring

## Seven connected AHA moments

1. Gmail import turns an empty tracker into 15 applications: 3 need attention, 4 waiting, 8 closed.
2. The assistant finds a real evidence gap and improves profile readiness.
3. Naukri import creates an editable, transparent preference profile.
4. Home ranks the next useful career action, not just another job list.
5. Interview preparation is personalized to the job, company, and candidate evidence.
6. A multi-source feed explains fit, readiness, pay, and company intelligence.
7. A Gmail-detected ₹28L Juspay offer is decoded and prepared for negotiation.

## Trust and honesty rules

- Gmail and Naukri are deterministic simulations of future integrations.
- Nothing is sent, submitted, or changed without review.
- Preference Match describes role alignment; Profile Readiness describes evidence captured.
- Arjun's Java ownership is never assumed. A correction branch asks him to confirm it.
- The UI must clearly identify source and freshness where it matters.

## Visual direction

Calm, premium mobile product UI with crisp information hierarchy, human copy, quiet depth, and high-quality transitions. Reference width is 390px and must remain sound from 360px through 430px.

Core palette: canvas `#f7f8fc`, surface `#ffffff`, ink `#17192b`, muted `#626985`, primary `#3f5cfb`, success `#168b63`, warning `#b96a12`, danger `#c94b58`. Use Figtree with robust system fallbacks.
