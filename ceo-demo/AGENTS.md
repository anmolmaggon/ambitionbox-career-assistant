# CEO Demo Workspace Rules

- Everything outside `ceo-demo/` is frozen and must only be read.
- Read `DEMO_CONTEXT.md`, `PRESERVATION_MANIFEST.md`, and the installed `ambitionbox-ceo-demo-builder` skill before extending this demo.
- Keep canonical people, companies, numbers, and journey facts in `src/data.js`.
- Keep Preference Match (role fit) separate from Profile Readiness (evidence completeness).
- Never invent candidate experience. Ask for confirmation or preserve an explicit gap.
- Never simulate sending an email or message without a review step.
- Complete loading, empty, success, return, reset, and reduced-motion states.
- Verify primary paths at 360px, 390px, and 430px widths.
- A phase is complete only after build, interaction, visual, and preservation checks pass.
